# Credits

The design of this site is our own. Structural and content-model ideas were
adapted from the projects below, all under permissive licences. Nothing here
copies their stylesheets.

- **[scholar-lite](https://github.com/fjd2004711/scholar-lite)** (MIT) — the
  shape of the content model: a `people` collection grouped by rank with
  per-person pages, alongside news, projects and highlighted publications.
- **[as-folio](https://github.com/dadangnh/as-folio)** (MIT) — the idea of
  centralising site-wide details in one place rather than repeating them per
  page.
- **[greenelab/lab-website-template](https://github.com/greenelab/lab-website-template)**
  (BSD-3-Clause) — the approach of generating publications from identifiers
  (here, ORCID) rather than maintaining them by hand.
- **[Utopia](https://utopia.fyi/)** — the fluid type and space method
  implemented in `src/lib/utopia.ts`.

Deliberately not used: any project without a licence file.
